7 Habits That Move Your Score
Why This Guide Exists

I see 30 credit applications every day. Twenty-nine get declined — not because people are irresponsible, but because nobody ever gave them a clear plan. These 7 habits are what separate clients who get approved in 6–8 months from the ones still stuck in 2 years.

01
Pay on time. Every time.
Highest Impact

One missed payment stays on your file for up to 7 years. Set up pre-authorized payments on every account — this week, not eventually.

Rule #1: Pre-authorized payments on every account. This single habit protects everything else you build.
02
Keep utilization under 30%
Highest Impact

High utilization signals risk — even if you pay in full every month. Keep every card under 30% of its individual limit, not just overall.

Quick win: Pay balances before your statement date. That's what gets reported — not your end-of-month balance.
03
Stop applying for credit
Medium Impact

Every application is a hard inquiry. Multiple hard inquiries signal financial stress and can drop your score 15–40 points. Do not apply for anything without contacting us first.

Credit Path Rule: Contact us before any application. We'll tell you exactly when and what to apply for.
04
Don't close old accounts
Medium Impact

A card you've had for 8 years is worth far more than a new one. Closing old accounts shrinks your credit history and raises your utilization. Keep them open.

05
Build to 3 network cards
Medium Impact

3 Visa, Mastercard, or Amex cards actively reporting to Equifax is one of the fastest ways to build your score. Secured cards count. Use each monthly, pay in full, stay under 30%.

Where to start: Neo Financial and Koho — no credit check required. See below.
06
Handle collections strategically
Medium Impact

Not all collections should be paid. The wrong move can restart the clock and extend damage by years.

Within 24 months of fall-off: Do not pay — let it age off.
Over 24 months, under $500: Pay with written deletion confirmation.
Over 24 months, over $500: Negotiate 30–40 cents, pay-for-delete in writing first.
Always contact us before taking action.
07
Monitor your bureau monthly
Ongoing

Errors on your bureau are common — wrong balances, duplicate collections, accounts that aren't yours. Every error costs you points. Use Borrowell for free weekly Equifax updates and dispute anything inaccurate immediately.
